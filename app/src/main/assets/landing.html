Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 15, 2026 | Publisher: JECON LLC (`com.tipsplit.ai`)

  • Calculator & Split Data: Bill totals, tax amounts, local split history, and configured payment handles (Venmo, CashApp, PayPal) are stored locally on your device via browser `localStorage` and are never sold to third parties.
  • Microphone & Voice Input (`RECORD_AUDIO`): When you explicitly initiate Voice Input and grant consent, audio is transcribed to populate calculator fields. If Cloud Voice Logging is active on Android, transcripts are stored in Google Cloud Firestore scoped strictly to your authenticated `userId`.
  • Camera & Receipt Images (`CAMERA`): Receipt images captured for the AI Scanner are processed transiently to extract subtotal and tax figures and are not permanently stored on our servers.
  • Data Deletion Requests: You may clear your local history at any time inside the app via Split History → Clear Local History, or request cloud log deletion via our support contact below.
